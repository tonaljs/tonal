tonal

A music theory library for JavaScript and TypeScript: notes, intervals, chords, scales, keys and more, as pure functions.

npm install tonal
Get startedTry the apps

Apps

Chord finder

Play or type some notes to get the chord name, or type a chord symbol to get its notes.

Scale chords

Pick a tonic and any of 92 scales to see the chord on each degree: triads, sevenths, or chords stacked in fourths or seconds.

Key explorer

Click a key on the circle of fifths to get its signature, scale and chords: diatonic chords with their functions, secondary and substitute dominants with their ii, chord scales, and the related keys. Major and the three minor scales.

Chart reader

Paste a chord chart to read every symbol: notes, family, complexity and roman numerals in a key, with applied dominants. Transpose it and play it.

Scale families

The seven modes of the major, melodic minor, harmonic minor and other scale families, side by side, and which of them fit a chord.

Scale harmonizer

Add voices in thirds, sixths or tenths that stay in a scale to a melody, and see how consonant each interval is.

Voicings

Voice a chord progression for piano: how many voices, which tensions, who plays the bass, rootless and So What templates, drop 2 and spread. Each chord led smoothly from the one before.

Rhythm patterns

Draw a rhythm on a circle, or pick a clave or a Euclidean rhythm, and see why some rhythms recur around the world: evenness, rhythmic oddity, deepness, off-beatness and distances, from Toussaint's The Geometry of Musical Rhythm. Play it with drums.

Note patterns

Play arpeggios and melodic sequences over a chord progression: an arpeggio order or a cell of indexes, over each chord's voicing, its chord tones or the scale, kept in a register and put on a rhythm.
