With tonal

tonal gives the pieces; the search that puts them together, its costs and the random choices live in the app (lib/walking-bass.ts).

How often a bass plays each kind of note, interval, approach and figure comes from our counts of FiloBass, 48 transcribed jazz walking bass lines (Jasper Riley and Simon Dixon, “FiloBass: A Dataset and Corpus Based Study of Jazz Basslines”, ISMIR 2023): rounded percentages, and a vocabulary of common four-note figures as degrees (like R-9-b3-3) with how often each is played before a chord change. No transcribed line is included. Unlike the rest of this site (AGPL-3.0), the figure table (lib/walking-bass-figures.ts) is not under its license: like the FiloBass data, it’s for non-commercial research use.
