Reharmonizer

Paste a chart and reharmonize it: the original on top with what each chord does in the key, and below a dropdown of substitutes on each chord, or one click for tritone subs, ii–Vs, secondary dominants or passing diminished chords. Hear one after the other, with a melody that follows the chords.

Try:
Reharmonize

In C major

The original on top, with what each chord does in the key (Key.analyzeChord; click one for every reading). Below, a dropdown on each chord with its substitutes (Key.substitutes); “A/B” plays one then the other, in a loop.
