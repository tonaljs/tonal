Rhythm patterns

Draw a rhythm on a circle, or pick a clave or a Euclidean rhythm, and see why some rhythms recur around the world: evenness, rhythmic oddity, deepness, off-beatness and distances, from Toussaint's The Geometry of Musical Rhythm. Lay it over a meter (6/8, 2+2+3/8) to hear its pulses, and play it with drums.

The six timelines

Toussaint's six distinguished timelines, five onsets in 16 steps. The distances are to your pattern: Hamming counts the steps that differ, swap how far the onsets move.
