Voicings

Voice a chord progression for piano: how many voices, which tensions, who plays the bass, rootless and So What templates, drop 2 and spread. Each chord led smoothly from the one before.

Try:
Styles:
Voices
Tensions
Bass
Voice leading
helda stepa 3rda leap

The voices move 6 semitones in total along the progression. Voicing each chord on its own, they would move 42.

Click a chord to hear it, see how its voices move, and give it its own options.
