`Chart.unroll` reads the form conventions of real lead sheets: an ending can start a section; `<D.C. al 2nd ending>` (1st, 2nd or 3rd, D.C. or D.S.; also `2nd End.`) plays that ending after the jump, and an ending written after its group is played by a jump to it; a `<Coda>` alone (no To Coda, no jump) is played once, after the last chorus; a `<Segno>` without a D.S. and a `<Fine>` without any jump are only signs. They change charts that were problems before, and two readings that were wrong: a second ending that starts a section is no longer skipped.
