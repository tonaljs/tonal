`Chart.unroll` reads the form conventions of real lead sheets: an ending can start a section, `<D.C. al 2nd ending>` (1st, 2nd or 3rd, D.C. or D.S.; also `2nd End.`) plays that ending after the jump, an ending written after its group is played by a jump to it, and a `<Segno>` without a D.S. is only a sign. They only change charts that were problems before.
