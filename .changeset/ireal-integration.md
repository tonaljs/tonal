New integration, `@tonaljs/ireal`: `IReal.parse(url)` reads an iReal Pro link (`irealb://` or `irealbook://`, one song or a playlist) into songs with their fields and a `Chart` each, as written, with chords placed by their cell; `IReal.chord(symbol)` gives the tonal symbol of an iReal Pro chord. Not part of `tonal`: install it beside it.
