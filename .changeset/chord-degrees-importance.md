`Chord.degreesByImportance` follows jazz voicing practice. The 7th now comes before an altered 5th (`Cm7b5` is `b3 b7 b5 1`), and the extension that names the chord comes before the root, with the implied extensions after it and the natural 5th last: `C13` is `3 b7 13 1 9 5` (it was `3 b7 9 1 5 13`), so four voices of a 13 chord keep the 13. Altered extensions come right after the one that names the chord.
