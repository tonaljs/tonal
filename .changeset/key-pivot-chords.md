`Key.pivotChords(from, to, { sevenths, chromatic })`: the chords two keys share (pivot chords for a modulation), with the numeral of each in both keys. Triads by default, 7th chords with `sevenths`; a minor key has the chords of its three forms. With `chromatic`, also the chords with a functional reading in the other key (D: V/V in C, V in G).
