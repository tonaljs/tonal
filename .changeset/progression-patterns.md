Named progressions: `Progression.patterns()` is a catalogue of the progressions of jazz (and some pop and blues), with their numerals and aliases, ordered by how common they are in a corpus of jazz standards; `Progression.find(chords, pattern)` finds one in any key, by name, alias or numerals; `Progression.fromPattern(pattern, tonic)` gives its chords in a key.
